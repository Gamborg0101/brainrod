# Stories — channel bible

**Audience:** 16–30, scrolling for a laugh. **Length:** 35–60 s. **Voice:** am_puck @ 1.10–1.15.

## Format
- Line 1 is the hook: a weird, specific situation with a number ("for 41 days", "$3,000", "every Tuesday").
- Escalate every 2–3 lines. Short sentences. One absurd twist near the end, then a punchline.
- End on a loop or a "Follow for part 2" only when a part 2 really exists.
- Fictional and first-person. No real people or brands, nothing mean about real groups.
- Write numbers as words in `text` so the voice reads them right.
