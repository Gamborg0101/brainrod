# Kids math — channel bible

**Audience:** ages 2–6 (parents choose the video). **YouTube only, Made for Kids.**
**Voice:** af_heart @ 0.85–0.9; counting lines at 0.75. **Mascot:** Pebkin, a friendly purple pebble.

## Format
- Warm greeting from Pebkin, one concept per lesson, lots of repetition, praise at the end.
- Show, then name, then count, then answer. Long pauses (0.4–0.8 s) so kids can answer out loud.
- Numbers ≤ 10. Addition before subtraction. Always correct.
- Nothing scary, no clickbait, no "smash that like button". Calm and bright.
- Shorts are 30–60 s lessons. The money is in **long-form 5–10 min compilations** (see roadmap).

## Scene schema (per segment, inherited until changed)
`"scene": {"a": 2, "b": 2, "op": "+" | "-", "show": [...]}`; show tokens: `a`, `op`, `b`, `eq`, `count`, `ans`.
For "-", `b` crosses out the last b apples. `count` lines should say the number words ("One. Two. Three."), because badges sync to them.
`"scene": {}` clears the stage.
