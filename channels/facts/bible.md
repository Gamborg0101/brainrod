# Facts — channel bible

**Audience:** 13–35, curious, likes "wait, what?" moments. **Length:** 30–50 s. **Voice:** am_eric @ 1.15.

## Format
- The hook promises a number of facts ("5 facts…", "3 animals that…"). Vary the framing every video.
- Each fact: `label` "Fact #n", a 1–2 word `big` keyword, one line stating the fact, an optional one-line twist.
- End by asking a question to get comments ("Which one broke your brain?").
- **Only true, well-documented facts.** Avoid famous myths (goldfish 3-second memory, the Great Wall seen from space, etc.).
- Theme the videos for variety: animals, space, food, history, human body, language, geography.

## Ideas backlog (themes)
- Animals with ridiculous superpowers (mantis shrimp punch, tardigrades)
- Food facts (honey never spoils, ketchup was sold as medicine)
- Space scale (neutron star teaspoon, Saturn would float)
- Human body (you're taller in the morning, stomach lining renews)
- History is weird (Cleopatra lived closer to the Moon landing than to the pyramids being built)
- Words with wild origins
